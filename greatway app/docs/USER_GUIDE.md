# USER GUIDE - Greatway Cooperative Member Management System

## Getting Started

### Login
1. Open the application
2. Enter your officer credentials
3. Click "Login"

## Main Features

### Dashboard
- Overview of all members and financial statistics
- Active loan count and total outstanding
- Total savings and shares value
- Recent activities feed

### Member Management
- View all members with filtering options
- Register new members
- Update member information
- View member financial summary
- Member status management (active, inactive, suspended)

### Financial Records

#### Savings
- Record member savings deposits
- View savings transaction history
- Calculate total member savings

#### Shares
- Record share purchases
- View share holdings
- Track share prices and quantities

#### Fees
- Record AGM fees
- Record administrative charges
- Track Ileya savings
- Record other subscription fees

### Loan Management

#### Loan Application
- Submit loan applications for members
- Multiple loan types: Personal, Business

#### Loan Eligibility
To be eligible for a loan, a member must:
1. Have been a member for at least 6 months
2. Have no defaulted loans
3. Have maximum 3 active loans
4. Loanable amount = 2x total savings (with at least 10% in shares)

#### Loan Approval
- Review pending loan applications
- Approve or reject applications
- Set interest rate and tenure

#### Loan Disbursement
- Disburse approved loans to members
- Generate disbursement receipts

#### Loan Repayment
- Record member loan repayments
- Track repayment progress
- Monitor overdue payments

### Reports & Dashboard
- Member performance analytics
- Loan portfolio summary
- Financial health indicators
- Payment schedules and history

## Common Tasks

### Registering a New Member
1. Go to Members → Add Member
2. Fill in member details
3. Record guarantor information
4. Click Save
5. Member code will be auto-generated

### Recording Savings
1. Go to Finances → Savings
2. Select member
3. Enter amount
4. Enter transaction date
5. Click Record

### Approving a Loan
1. Go to Loans → Pending Applications
2. Click on application
3. Review member eligibility
4. Verify loanable amount
5. Set terms (interest rate, tenure)
6. Click Approve

### Recording Loan Repayment
1. Go to Loans → Active Loans
2. Select the loan
3. Click "Record Repayment"
4. Enter amount and payment date
5. Click Record

## Tips & Best Practices

### Data Entry
- Always verify member information before saving
- Use consistent date formats
- Double-check financial amounts
- Add descriptions for all transactions

### Loan Management
- Always check member eligibility before approving
- Verify savings and share requirements
- Monitor loan repayment schedules
- Flag overdue payments immediately

### Member Follow-up
- Review inactive members monthly
- Monitor high-risk loan accounts
- Send payment reminders before due dates
- Conduct annual KYC verification

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Login fails**
- Check internet connection
- Verify username and password
- Contact system administrator

**Cannot register member**
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check for duplicate member codes
- Verify email and phone format

**Loan application rejected**
- Check 6-month membership requirement
- Verify no defaulted loans
- Confirm savings requirement met
